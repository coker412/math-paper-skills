## Check function before editing

For a proposed deletion or weakening of an explanation, reminder or contrast:

1. Read the affected claim and adjacent argument. Identify both the mathematical content and the reader's task: decoding a formula, recalling a convention, following a dependency, or distinguishing conclusions. A useful explanation need not add a new theorem or be logically indispensable. `Note that`, `recall`, negation and repeated wording are not defects by themselves.
2. Choose keep, rephrase, move or delete within the authorized scope. Keep clear, useful prose unchanged. If only the framing is padded, edit the framing and retain the explanation. Delete redundancy only when context already supplies its content and reading function; move useful information when its current position is the problem. If supplied context cannot settle its role, retain it and flag the specific uncertainty rather than inventing a reason to remove it.
3. Read the edited passage through the next use. Check mathematical meaning and reader guidance separately: does the reader still know which fact licenses the step, how a formula is interpreted, or what distinction matters? Restore a lost explanation even if the shortened text remains formally true. Keep this check internal for routine edits; report disputed changes briefly when needed, without a new audit file.

A true but tangential literature fact is normally a content-selection issue, not evidence of defensive tone. Preserve the difference between an argument not establishing a stronger conclusion and that conclusion being false. The function check limits the original cleaner's stylistic choices; it does not require rewriting every sentence or running another mandatory pass.

Follow the [stage boundaries](humanizer-interface.md#coordinate-editing-stages): review with anti-defensive writing before freezing the selected passage for Humanizer review. Both reviews are default completion steps; neither requires a rewrite. Profiles remain optional, and no repeated cleaner loop is required. Follow the original skill's procedure rather than duplicating it here.
