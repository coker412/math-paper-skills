# Delivery checks

Use these checks for the actual requested scope and affected dependencies. Repository completion requirements still apply. Reuse working context and existing records; no separate checklist, per-paragraph report or rewrite quota is required.

## Mathematical fidelity and artifact checks

Compare the final artifact with the source and inherited author decisions: scope, template, notation, declarations and destination. For revisions inspect the actual diff. Check affected claims' hypotheses, domains, quantifiers, constants and evidence status; compare new symbols with the [notation gate](mathematical-integrity.md#notation-and-necessity). Check proof dependencies, omitted difficult steps and citations supporting changed claims. Preserve necessary scope while stating established results directly.

Check changed LaTeX and affected versions with the project's build procedure. Inspect relevant rendered pages. Include figure/caption claims, accessibility and reproducibility when touched; submission materials against the manuscript and venue requirements when requested. File placement and clean logs do not establish mathematical or stylistic correctness.

## Exposition checks

For first drafts, deliver the promised prose, statements and proofs, and assess the applicable [quality dimensions](exposition-and-structure.md#what-a-writing-improvement-must-accomplish). An accurate outline is not a completed manuscript. For revisions identify the reading difficulty resolved and costs in length, notation or navigation; revert gratuitous changes.

For proof exposition, check that the target stays visible, objects remain identifiable, technical detours return to the formulas they justify, and the closing inference uses the same constructed objects. Factor long arguments at real interfaces without fragmenting short calculations. For introduction work, perform its [read-back and source reconciliation](exposition-and-structure.md#introduction); reconcile abstract and conclusion with the body when affected. For structural edits, check information selection and distinct paragraph roles before cleanup. For removed explanations, check [reader function through the next use](anti-defensive-interface.md#check-function-before-editing), not just truth of the shortened sentence.

## Execution and handoff

Complete the entrypoint's [review stages](../SKILL.md#execute-the-requested-review), including both original language reviews and final semantic read-back. Tie coverage to the delivered source/version and retain concrete changed or retained passages with reasons. A later edit reopens its affected scope; unread and unresolved material stays explicitly unverified.

Report paths, useful changes, checks and remaining gaps concisely. Distinguish observed execution, mathematical comparison and editorial judgment. A loaded skill, unchanged proof hash, clean build or self-declared completion does not prove the other dimensions. Behavioral evaluation requires actual traces and artifacts; prose-quality claims remain editorial judgments without reader evidence.
